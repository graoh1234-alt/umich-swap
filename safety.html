Safety first

Meet up smart: simple guidelines to keep buying and selling safe on campus.

Join waitlist Browse listings

Meetup tips

  • Meet in public, well-lit areas (Union, Diag, library entrances).
  • Bring a friend for high-value items.
  • Use cashless payment when possible.
  • Inspect items before exchanging payment.
  • If anything feels off, cancel the meetup.

Verification

  • Student-only access via UMich email verification (demo concept).
  • Report suspicious behavior and block users (future feature).
